1. Signing In & Roles

Open the portal and click Sign in with Google, using your authorised school Gmail account. If sign-in fails, it usually means your account hasn't been added as staff yet — contact an Admin.

Every account has exactly one role, set by an Admin under Admin Settings → Manage Staff:

  • Admin — full access, including catalog, buildings, staff management, and Archive.
  • Chairman / Principal — final-stage approvers for escalated requests.
  • Coordinator — first-stage approver for both Item and Service requests.
  • Staff cum Portal User — can raise requests, receive purchases, and manage day-to-day inventory, but cannot approve or edit the catalog.
  • Staff — read-only / requesting access only, depending on what an Admin enables.

Your role decides which tabs and buttons you see — this is normal, not a bug. See the Roles & Permissions Reference for the full breakdown.

2. Dashboard

The Home page is your at-a-glance status screen. Nothing here needs to be clicked into to understand it — a one-second scan should tell you if anything needs attention.

Top KPI row

  • Inventory Items — total distinct catalog items currently tracked in stock.
  • Low Stock Alerts — items at or below their reorder threshold right now.
  • Requests Pending — all requests anywhere in the portal still awaiting an approval decision.
  • Awaiting My Action — everything specifically waiting on you, based on your role, added up across every queue below.

Pending Work

Six cards, one per queue. A card only counts items that are actually yours to act on (based on your role) — an "all clear" card doesn't mean nothing is happening company-wide, just that nothing there needs you specifically.

Example If you're a Coordinator, "Pending Approvals" shows purchase requests waiting on you, but "Item Receipt" only lights up once you (or anyone with receiving rights) actually needs to confirm a delivery.

Procurement & Expenses at a Glance

Rolling counts and totals — requests raised today/this week, and money actually spent today/this week/this month. "Expenses" only count recorded purchases and closed services — a request still sitting in Approval contributes ₹0 until it's actually purchased.

Recent Activity

A scrollable strip of everything that happened in the last 24 hours — not just the last few records. If nothing happened today, it shows a clean "No recent activity" message instead of stale entries from days ago.

The Dashboard refreshes itself in the background roughly every 30 seconds, and does an extra check the moment you land on it — so if a colleague approves or closes something, you'll see it update without needing to reload the page.

3. Inventory

Inventory is where all physical stock — consumables and assets — actually lives, independent of how it arrived (bought through Procurement, or added as pre-existing stock).

3.1 Browse by Category

The default tab — a grid of every category (Stationery, Kitchen/Pantry, Electrical, Furniture, etc.), each showing item count. Click into any category to see its item list, current stock, and (for Admins) + Add Item to create a brand-new catalog item.

3.2 Consumables tab

A flat table of every consumable across all categories, each with a Consume button — the way you record stock actually being used up (e.g. handed out, used in class, used up in the kitchen).

Example — recording use The kitchen used 2 litres of Milk this morning. Open Inventory → Consumables, find Milk, click Consume, enter Quantity 2, optionally note the Building/Room and a purpose ("Staff breakfast"), and submit. Milk's stock count drops by 2 immediately, and it's logged permanently in that item's Stock Ledger.

+ Add Existing Stock is for stock that's physically already at school but never went through the Procurement workflow (e.g. donated items, or stock found during a physical count). Pick the catalog item it matches, enter the quantity, and optionally a purchase/reference date, reference note, remarks, and a photo.

Import CSV lets you add many existing-stock records at once instead of one at a time:

  1. Click Import CSV, then Download CSV Template to get the exact column format.
  2. Fill in ITEM, QUANTITY (required) and Purchase/Reference Date, Reference, Remarks, PHOTO (all optional) — ITEM must match an existing catalog item exactly by name.
  3. If any rows have a PHOTO filename, also select those image files (in addition to the CSV) using "Select Photos" — they're matched to rows by filename.
  4. Click Parse & Preview. Every row is validated and shown in a table — invalid rows are flagged in red with the specific problem (unknown item, bad quantity, bad date, missing photo).
  5. Import Stock only becomes clickable once every row is valid — nothing is imported while any row still has an error.
Example — bulk import A stock-take finds 10 reams of A4 paper and 5 bottles of Fevicol that were never logged. Instead of adding them one at a time, build a two-row CSV, import it, fix any flagged rows, and import both in one go — they behave exactly like manually-added stock afterwards.

3.3 Assets tab

Three sub-tabs:

  • Installed Assets — everything currently placed somewhere in the school, grouped by item, each with a Dispose action.
  • Spare Assets — assets sitting in storage, not yet installed anywhere, each with an Install Asset action (choose a building, optionally a room).
  • Disposed-Off Assets — permanently removed assets, kept for audit history.
Example — installing a spare fan Spare Assets shows "Ceiling Fan — Qty Spare: 3". Click Install Asset, choose Quantity 1, Building "Main Building", Room "Room 12", add a remark if useful, and submit. The fan moves from Spare (now 2) to Installed, tagged to that exact location.

Updating an asset's location: open the asset's item detail page (click its name from any Assets list) — each active installation card has an Update Location button. This is for assets that physically move after being installed (a fan relocated to a different classroom, for example) — it keeps full history at both the old and new location rather than silently overwriting where it used to be.

3.4 Item detail page

Clicking any item name opens its full record: current stock, reorder threshold, average purchase cost, photo, installation/disposal history (assets), purchase history, the complete Stock Ledger, pending requests, attachments, and a full audit log.

Stock Ledger & Adjustments

The Stock Ledger is the permanent, chronological record of every quantity change for that item — Goods Receipt, Install, Dispose, Move, Issue (consumption) — each with a running balance. It's the one place to see exactly how an item's current stock number was arrived at.

Record Adjustment (Admin only) is the correction tool for anything that doesn't fit those normal flows — a physical recount finding a discrepancy, damage discovered later, or fixing a data-entry mistake. Enter a signed quantity (+5 or -3) and a required reason; it's logged to the ledger just like any other transaction, never a silent edit.

Adjustments should be the exception, not the routine way stock changes — use Consume, Receipt, Install, or Dispose for anything that actually happened through a normal process, so the audit trail stays meaningful.

3.5 Low Stock Alerts

Set the Reorder Threshold on any item via its Edit button (in its category's item list). Once stock falls to or below that number, a red alert banner appears at the top of the Dashboard for everyone. It clears automatically once stock is topped back up above the threshold — and will reappear on its own if it drops low again later, so it's never a one-time thing. Only an Admin can manually dismiss an active alert.

4. Item Procurement

The full lifecycle for buying items — from request to the stock actually landing in Inventory. Every stage is permanently tracked.

4.1 New Request

  1. Choose a Category, then an Item from that category's locked catalog (you can't type a free-text item name — this keeps stock names consistent).
  2. Enter Quantity, Priority, and a Reason.
  3. Add more items to the same request with + Add Another Item if needed.
  4. Submit — the request either goes straight to Purchase Assignment (if that category doesn't require approval) or into the Approval queue.
Example A teacher needs 5 whiteboard markers. New Request → Category "Stationery Items" → Item "Whiteboard Marker" → Quantity 5 → Reason "Classroom Requirements" → Submit. Stationery doesn't require approval, so it goes straight to Purchase Assignment.

4.2 Approvals

Requests needing approval land with whoever holds the Coordinator role. From there: Approve (moves on to Purchase Assignment), Decline (a comment is required, explaining why), or Escalate to the Principal for a second opinion.

4.3 Purchase Assignment

Once approved, someone records the actual purchase: supplier, invoice number, purchase date, unit price, tax, and optionally a bill/receipt photo. Unit Price must be a genuine positive amount — zero or negative values are rejected, since ₹0 isn't a real purchase.

4.4 Item Receipt

Confirms the goods have physically arrived, with condition, remarks, and an optional photo. What happens next depends on the item type:

  • Consumables go straight into Inventory → Consumables' current stock — the request is complete at this point, no further step needed.
  • Assets move to Item Issue / Return to decide where they go.

4.5 Item Issue / Return

Only shown for assets. Decide whether the received asset is installed immediately at a location, or kept as spare stock for later.

4.6 Purchase Orders & All Requests

Purchase Orders lists every generated PO, printable individually. All Requests is the full, filterable history of every Item Procurement request ever raised, whatever stage it's at.

5. Service Procurement

For work done by outside contractors or vendors — repairs, maintenance, installations — as opposed to buying physical stock.

5.1 New Request

  1. Choose whether the service is tied to a specific asset, or general.
  2. Choose a Category and Sub-category (e.g. "Maintenance & Repairs → Electrical").
  3. Set Priority and an Expected Completion Date.
  4. Describe the problem in Remarks, and optionally attach a photo — click "Preview" beside the file picker to check the photo before submitting.
Example A classroom fan is making a grinding noise. New Request → General → Category "Maintenance & Repairs" / "Electrical" → Priority Medium → Remarks "Fan making a loud grinding noise, needs inspection" → attach a photo of the fan → Submit.

5.2 Approvals

Same Coordinator → Principal approval flow as Item Procurement. Every approver on the request can see the requester's remark and attached photo directly on the approval card, not just a summary.

5.3 Service Details & Receipts

Once approved, the request sits here until the work is actually done and confirmed. Open Confirm Service Receipt:

  • Checked By — must be explicitly chosen; nobody is pre-selected, since the system never assumes who actually checked the work.
  • Service Charges — must be a genuine positive amount to close the service; ₹0, blank, or negative values are rejected.
  • Total Amount auto-calculates from Charges + Taxes as you type.
  • Service Status — Satisfactory, Not Satisfactory, or Under Observation.

Three outcomes:

ActionResult
Close Service (Satisfactory)Request closes permanently, counted in expenditure.
Close Service (Not Satisfactory)Recorded, but stays active here for follow-up rather than closing.
Mark for ObservationStays open for a trial period before final closure — Service Charges isn't required yet, since the job isn't finished.

5.4 All Service Requests & Generate Report

All Service Requests is the full filterable history. Any individual request has a Generate Report option producing a PDF with the complete record — request details, the requester's remark, the actual uploaded photo (if any) under an "Attachment" heading, and the full receipt/closure details.

6. Admin Settings

Admin-only configuration for the whole portal.

6.1 Manage Staff

Add staff, set their role, and toggle active/inactive. A staff member's email here is what all approval and receipt actions get attributed to, and what email alerts (see Alerts & Notifications) are sent to.

6.2 Manage Buildings

The building/room list used everywhere assets get a location — Item Issue, Install Asset, and Update Location.

6.3 Manage Categories & Items

The locked catalog every request and stock entry is built from. For each item you can set:

  • Item Type — Consumable, Asset, or Repair-type — controls which workflow the item follows.
  • Value Classification — Low / Medium / High / Emergency.
  • Approval requirement — toggle whether requests for this item need Coordinator approval at all.
New categories/items can be added at the bottom of each category card. Deleting an item here doesn't affect existing historical requests/stock — they keep their name as a permanent record.

6.4 Manage Service Categories

Mirrors Categories & Items, but for Service Procurement's categories/sub-categories and their approval requirement.

6.5 Archive

Anything deleted (categories, items, staff) lands here rather than disappearing — restorable at any time.

7. Search

The search box in the top bar looks across inventory items (by name or item code), categories, procurement requests (by item name, code, or request number), and Purchase Orders — all at once, live as you type.

Example Typing PR-2026-0024 jumps straight to that request's detail page, regardless of which module or stage it's currently sitting in.

8. Alerts & Notifications

Two kinds of automatic notification, in addition to what you see live inside the portal:

  • Low stock banner (on the Dashboard) — see 3.5 Low Stock Alerts.
  • Email alerts — when a request needs approval, when an approval decision is made (approved/declined/escalated), and when a receipt is confirmed, an email goes out to everyone who's been involved in that request's history, plus all Admins. A daily expenditure summary is also emailed each morning to everyone holding an approver role (Coordinator/Principal/Chairman).
Email alerts only reach people whose email is correctly set under Manage Staff — if you're not receiving them, check that first with an Admin.

10. Tips & Troubleshooting

  • A dropdown or field looks locked/blank — try refreshing the page. If it persists, tell an Admin exactly which item/screen it was on.
  • I can't see a queue I expect to act on — check your role (see above); most action buttons are role-gated by design.
  • Numbers on the Dashboard look out of date — it self-refreshes roughly every 30 seconds, and again the moment you open it; a manual page reload always forces the latest.
  • A PDF/report seems to be missing a photo — only requests that actually had a photo uploaded will show one; nothing is faked or left as a broken image.
  • I made a mistake on stock count — don't try to "fix" it by editing history; use Record Adjustment on that item's page instead, with a clear reason.
  • For anything not covered here, use the Contact page in the portal, or ask an Admin directly.
